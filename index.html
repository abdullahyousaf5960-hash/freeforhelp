The idea behind HP596

Built to make digital work more accessible.

A little help can open big opportunities.

HP596 was created by Abdullah Yousaf with one clear goal: help students, independent creators and online workers discover useful digital tools without feeling that every task requires expensive software.

Here you can explore tools for productivity, design, video creation, learning and more — all gathered in one convenient directory. Many services offer free plans or free features, though some may have limits, paid upgrades or eligibility requirements.

I also build attractive, responsive websites for shops, restaurants, personal brands and growing businesses. If you have an idea, I can help turn it into a website tailored to your needs and budget.
